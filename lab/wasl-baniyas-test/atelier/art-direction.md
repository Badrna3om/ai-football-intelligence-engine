# Art Direction — TACTIC Wasl × Baniyas

## Subject
Premium Arabic post-match football recap for Baniyas 0–2 Al Wasl, built around real goal footage and verified match data.

## Palette
- #0B0E12 near-black matte base
- #F5F5F2 warm white typography
- #E4B23D restrained TACTIC gold accent
- #343941 steel tactical-grid support
- #BFC4CB cool neutral for secondary information

## Type personality
Arabic uses the official Thmanyah family only. Headlines are bold, confident and broadcast-like; supporting text is restrained and compact. Latin/numerals remain clean and geometric.

## Motion character
Fast broadcast energy with controlled cinematic weight. Use spring entries, sharp 8–14 frame impacts, 18–28 frame settles, and slower breathing motion for award/stat moments. No generic slide-in card rhythm.

## Layout & rhythm
Real footage dominates the goal scenes. Graphics live in safe margins and lower-thirds, never over the decisive action. The stat sequence changes composition every beat rather than repeating one card shell.

## Signature device
A tactical gold "match pulse" line: a thin gold path that sweeps across the frame and transforms between a pitch line, xG meter, shot trail and rating ring. It appears only at key transitions and the MOTM reveal.

## Scene distinctness
1. Intro — opposing crests, score tension, vertical tactical tunnel.
2. Goal 1 — full-width real footage with compact scorer lower-third.
3. Goal 2 — full-width real footage, different lower-third entrance and pulse treatment.
4. Possession — split horizontal control field.
5. xG — twin vertical energy meters.
6. Shots — tactical shot trails across a mini-pitch.
7. Shots on target — goal-mouth target grid with animated dots.
8. Big chances — penalty-box pulse zones.
9. MOTM — Cameron Puertas cutout as hero, rating ring and staggered stats.
10. Outro — score lockup and TACTIC identity.

## Anti-references
Do not resemble a generic Instagram sports template, a static infographic, a slideshow, or the previous prototype with large empty blurred margins and oversized lower-thirds.
